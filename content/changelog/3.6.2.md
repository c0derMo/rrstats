### No spoilers please!

Results and maps of matches within the last day are hidden by default on the tournament pages.
This can be configured between "Show all", "Hide last day", "Hide last week", and "Hide all", by clicking the eye button at the top right of the matches table.
The full result can always be found in the details dialog, opened by the "..." button next to each match, or on the players pages.
